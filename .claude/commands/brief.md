Umer said: **$1**

Do not build anything yet. This is the first 10%.

Produce:
1. **Goal** — one sentence, in outcome terms not activity terms
2. **Scope** — what is in, and explicitly what is out
3. **Constraints** — from `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` that bear on this
4. **Definition of done** — testable, with the gate that proves it
5. **Assumptions I am making** — the decisions I will take myself unless overruled
6. **What only you can supply** — data, judgement, or access I cannot substitute

Ask at most one clarifying question, and only if the answer would change the shape of the
build rather than a detail inside it. Then wait for confirmation.
