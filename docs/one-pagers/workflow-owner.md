## What you're worried about

"Am I being replaced."

## Said plainly, because the dancing is what you'd distrust

Naib drafts. You approve. Every enquiry — email, WhatsApp, form, voicemail — gets read,
qualified against your criteria, and turned into a proposal draft within minutes, 24/7. It
never sends anything. Your job changes from writing {{CURRENT_ENQUIRIES_PER_WEEK}} replies a
week from scratch to reviewing drafts in one queue: approve, edit, or reject, each in a few
seconds once you trust the pattern.

## What the queue actually looks like

Every pending item shows the full draft, the lead it's replying to, and one click each to
approve / edit / reject. Nothing is hidden behind a second screen. A trace button on every item
shows exactly why Naib decided what it decided — reasons, not just an answer.

## What happens when it's not sure

It stops and hands the lead to you with a written reason — low confidence, an existing client,
legal language, conflicting signals. It does not guess and hope. A confident wrong answer costs
more than an escalation, so escalation is the default whenever it isn't sure.

## What you get back

Time. The busywork of a first-pass reply on every enquiry — including the ones that turn out to
be spam or out of scope — moves off your desk. The judgment calls stay yours, because they were
never Naib's to make.

## The kill switch is yours too

It's on the dashboard, one click, no waiting for support. If something looks wrong, you stop it
yourself, immediately, and ask questions after.
